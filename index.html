Automation Upload

A desktop tool that turns a short topic into a finished vertical video — script, voiceover, and captions — held for review before anything is published.

Automation Upload runs locally on the creator's own computer. It writes a short script for a chosen topic, generates a voiceover, and assembles a captioned, vertical-format video. Every video is saved to a local folder first; nothing is published until the creator reviews and approves it.

How it works

1

Choose a topic

Pick a subject and a target length. A short script is written automatically for that topic.

2

Generate voice and video

Text-to-speech audio and background footage are combined into a finished, captioned vertical video.

3

Review

Every video is saved to a local folder for review. Nothing is published automatically.

4

Publish

Once the creator approves a video, it is posted to their connected TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Automation Upload connects only to the TikTok account of the person who installs and authorizes it. It does not access any other TikTok user's data, and nothing is published without that person's review and approval.

Privacy policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

This policy explains what Automation Upload does with data when it is connected to a TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.

Data we access

Only what is needed to publish a video to the account holder's own TikTok profile: the OAuth access token and the basic profile information returned by TikTok's creator-info endpoint. We do not access, read, or collect data belonging to any other TikTok user.

Data we store

Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the device running the app. They are not transmitted to any third-party server.

Video content

Scripts, audio, and video files are generated and stored locally. They are only sent to TikTok's servers at the moment the account holder chooses to publish a video.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't use data for anything beyond publishing the account holder's own videos.
